# Fitness Tracker

- **ID:** `fitness-tracker`
- **Status:** `idea`
- **Visibility:** `public`
- **Platform:** Google AI Studio, Google Sheets

## Goal

A lightweight personal app for logging workouts, charting progress over time, and tracking personal records without bloated fitness-app friction.

## Current state

- Idea phase; listed on `/portfolio` under **Play & ideas**.
- Existing notes and assets live in Google Drive under `/fitness`.

## Next steps

- [ ] Decide whether to prototype v1 in Google AI Studio backed by Google Sheets or as a small standalone web app.
- [ ] Sketch the minimal data model: workout date, movement, sets, reps, weight, and notes.
- [ ] Build a fast mobile-first log screen for phone use between sets.

## Knowledge capture & decisions

- Keep input speed the top priority: logging a set should take a few taps, not a multi-step form.
- Google Sheets makes a good lightweight backend for personal logs and charts before deciding if a real database is needed.
